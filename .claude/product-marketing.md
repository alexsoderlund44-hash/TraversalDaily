# Product marketing context: Traversle

Read by the vendored marketingskills skills (`programmatic-seo`, `schema`, `referrals`, `social`, `launch`, `directory-submissions`, `ai-seo`, `influencer-marketing`). The full research lives in the project's marketing data folder (`/mnt/project-files/marketing/`, see `marketing/README.md`); this is the short version.

- **Product:** Traversle (traversledaily.com), a free daily browser travel-route puzzle. Same start, destination, budget, deadline and twist for everyone each day; chain trains, buses, ferries, rideshares and flights; one official run; the planner's optimal route is revealed after. Weekday series: Open Road Mon, Southern Crossing Tue, Island Hopper Wed, Road Trip Thu, Mix It Up Fri, Eurorail Sat, Grand Tour Sun.
- **Business:** today's puzzle is free; Traversle + is a $2.99 one-time purchase for the archive. No subscription, no ads.
- **Audience:** daily-puzzle players (Wordle, Travle, MapTap habits), overland/budget travel fans (Interrail, Race Across the World, Jet Lag: The Game), geography hobbyists.
- **Positioning:** "beat the planner"; mixed transport + budget + a daily twist. Closest competitor: FlightQ (flightq.app). Do not reuse its "Daily Travel Planning & Budget Game" phrasing.
- **Owner:** Alex, one person, marketing graduate. Budget $0 by default; organic only (SEO, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Reddit, later email). No paid ads unless asked.
- **Rules for every recommendation:** label claims FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS; never invent volumes, traffic or engagement numbers; skip basic marketing explanations.
- **Decided against:** programmatic city-to-city route pages (doorway / scaled-content risk; fares are modelled, not real travel advice), today's-answer pages, GA4 (cookie consent), self-assigned ratings in structured data, affiliate or paid referral programs for now.
- **Structured data:** WebSite + Organization on the homepage only, until real ratings exist.
