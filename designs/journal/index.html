Three dials, one score

Your Traversle score blends how much you spent, how long the journey takes, and how quickly you decided, measured against the best routes that fit the day's mission. A cheap route that crawls loses to a balanced one, and so does a fast route that blows the budget.

Flight prices and times are averages of real fares for the day's cities, refreshed every morning. Land and sea legs are modelled from distance and calibrated to those fares.

When does the puzzle change?

At 00:00 UTC, the same moment for everyone.

Can I play more than once?

Replay as practice as often as you like, and replay the last week of puzzles from the archive. Only your first submission of the day counts.

Why is there never a direct route?

Because then there'd be nothing to plan. The budget is set so the obvious hop doesn't fit either. Every day needs real stops.

What is the planner's route?

The route the puzzle was built around: the best balance of money and time that fits the mission, usually using the hidden deals. You see it after you submit.

Where do the prices come from?

Flights are averaged from real one-way economy fares. Trains, buses, rideshares, ferries and cars are modelled from distance and calibrated to those fares.
