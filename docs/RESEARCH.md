# Daily geography games: what works, and what TraversleDaily borrows

Sources: Travle (travle.earth, Capitalle guides), Worldle, Globle, Flagle, MapTap, GeoGrid/GeoRiddle, Krillion, NPR's 2026 daily-puzzle roundup.

| Game | Core loop | What makes it stick | Borrowed into TraversleDaily |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Travle | Chain countries from A to B. Score = guesses over the shortest route ("perfect", "+1"). Green/orange/grey feedback. Hints. Past dailies replayable. | A single legible number against a known optimum. | "vs planner" delta on the result and share text; hints that cost clock time; past puzzles. |
| Worldle / Flagle | Six (or five) guesses with distance + direction clues. | A hard cap creates tension and makes streaks mean something. | Three undos per run instead of unlimited backtracking. |
| Globle | Unlimited guesses, heat-map colour feedback, stats panel (games won, streak, max streak, average). | The stats modal people screenshot. | Personal stats pop-up with rating distribution, streak and max streak, from the result and the header. |
| MapTap | Five rounds, later rounds multiplied, badges, streaks, leaderboard. | Escalation inside one session. | Rating tiers and XP already; weekly rhythm of twists is the next step (docs/TWIST-IDEAS.md). |
| GeoGrid / GeoRiddle | Fill a 3×3 grid; rare answers score more. | Rewarding the unobvious answer. | Parked: a "road less travelled" bonus once real player data exists. |
| Krillion | Seven prompts, 25-second timer each, rarity scoring, free, archive. | Time pressure per decision. | The decision clock; hints add seconds to it. |

Layout patterns shared by all of them: one column, play area visible instantly, a "?" and a stats icon in the header, how-to-play shown on the first visit, emoji share line, countdown to the next puzzle, no account needed.
