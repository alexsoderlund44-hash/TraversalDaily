Score estimate—

    One world, one puzzle a day

    Every day at 00:00 UTC, everyone gets the same start and destination. There is never a direct flight, so the route is yours to invent.

    Real fares

    Flight prices and times are averages of real one-way fares for the day's start, hubs and destination, refreshed daily. Trains, buses, ferries and cars are modelled from distance and calibrated to those fares.

    Three dials

    Money spent, hours on the move, and seconds spent deciding. The score blends all three. Winning on one dial alone won't take you to the top.

    One official run

    Your first submission of the day is the one that counts. Replay as practice as often as you like.
