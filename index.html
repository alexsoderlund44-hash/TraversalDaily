How to play

One route a day. One attempt.

  1. Check the mission. A budget, a deadline and one twist. The clock starts when you do.
  2. Build the route. Tap a city, pick how you get there, repeat. Anything that breaks the mission is greyed out.
  3. Find the deals. Three legs are secretly cheap. Two of them are on the planner's route. You only see a deal when you look.
  4. Submit and compare. The planner's route appears next to yours. Earn a rating, bank the XP, keep the streak.

Your score: money spent, time taken and how fast you decided. Your first run is the one that counts. Practice as often as you like.
